01 About Nebras

Rooted in the
Qur'an & Sunnah.

Nebras is a nonprofit Da'wah organization based in Lincoln, Nebraska, dedicated to spreading the message of Islam upon the guidance of the Qur'an and Sunnah, as understood by the first three righteous generations.

Initially founded in 2019, Nebras officially established itself in 2025, taking its first big step toward its mission of providing clear and authentic Islamic education to all.

We strengthen the Muslim community and serve as a source of guidance for those seeking to learn about Islam — through distributing educational materials, hosting community programs, and supporting Muslim inmates across Nebraska.

03 Prison Da'wah

Every soul deserves
authentic guidance.

One of Nebras's most impactful programs reaches inside Nebraska's correctional facilities. We believe that no circumstance should cut someone off from authentic Islamic knowledge.

We provide incarcerated Muslims and those curious about Islam with vetted books, written correspondence, and ongoing mentorship. Your donations make this possible.

Our Team

The People Behind Nebras

A dedicated team united by knowledge, sincerity, and commitment to authentic Islamic principles — serving the Nebraska Muslim community and beyond.

أج
Co-Founder & Admin
Abu Juwairiyah
Co-Founder · Administrator

Abu Juwairiyah is the co-founder of Nebras and currently serves as an admin for the organization. Born and raised in Lincoln, Nebraska, AJ has dedicated over a decade to serving the Muslim communities of Lincoln and Omaha.

He has been actively involved in da'wah, education, and community development, volunteering in the local masjid and its weekend school for more than ten years. In addition, he served as a board member for five consecutive years, gaining valuable leadership experience. His work reflects a commitment to community growth through knowledge, service, and adherence to authentic Islamic principles.

AJ earned his Bachelor's degree in Speech-Language Pathology from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL) prior to pursuing his journey of seeking knowledge at the Islamic University of Madinah. He has completed a Diploma in Arabic Language and his Bachelor's from the Faculty of Sharī'ah. While in Madinah, AJ has had the opportunity and privilege to benefit from major scholars and complete books with various mashāyikh. His journey of seeking knowledge served as the foundation and driving force behind the establishment of Nebras.

أب
Co-Founder & Treasurer
Abu Salma
Co-Founder · Treasurer

Abu Salma E. Boston is Co-founder and Treasurer of Nebras, where he serves as the organization's principal steward of financial integrity and accountability. In this role, he oversees income and expense tracking, budget development, financial reporting, and regulatory compliance — ensuring that all funds are managed in strict accordance with Islamic principles and nonprofit governance standards.

His work reflects a strong commitment to amānah (trustworthiness), transparency, and sustainability, providing the Board and Trustees with clear, data-driven financial guidance to support responsible growth and community impact.

Professionally, Abu Salma is a Certified Lead Auditor with extensive experience in Quality Management Systems (QMS), Halal Management Systems (HMS), Integrated Management Systems (IMS), and regulatory compliance across a variety of service sectors in the US. He currently serves as a QMS Lead Auditor and HMS Sharī'ah Auditor. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Islamic Studies and a Master's degree in Islamic Commercial Law (2026), bringing together technical expertise and classical Islamic scholarship to ensure Nebras operates with both professional and ethical rigor.

أس
Secretary
Abu Sulaiman
Secretary

Abu Sulaiman serves as the Secretary of Nebras and is deeply dedicated to the growth and well-being of the Muslim community in Lincoln. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Horticulture and his Master's in Community and Regional Planning from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, along with a graduate certificate in Public Administration and Management from the University of Nebraska at Omaha.

For over a decade, Abu Sulaiman has been an active volunteer in the Lincoln and Omaha communities, including more than five years serving as Vice-Principal and Principal at the weekend Islamic school. His leadership in service is guided by the values and methodology of the first generations in Islam — emphasizing sincerity, knowledge, and community stewardship.

نِبْرَاس

What is Nebras?

Nebras /nibˈrɑːs/ is an Arabic word meaning "lamp," "beacon," or "guiding light." It reflects our mission of bringing the light of the Qur'an and Sunnah to the people of Nebraska through education, outreach, and service.

Just as a beacon illuminates the path, we strive to share authentic Islamic knowledge that enlightens hearts, minds, and communities.
